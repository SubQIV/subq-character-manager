How to run the test
  1. On each device, give it a name below, such as DM laptop or Dan's phone.
  2. Round 1 uses Picked files only. On device A, sign in with your account and tap Create test table. Then enter the second account's email and tap Share. If sharing fails, share the folder from Google Drive as Editor.
  3. On device B, sign in with the second account. Tap Pick from Drive, find SubQ Table (phase 0 test) under shared items, pick it, then tap Scan table.
  4. Open Howerd on both devices. Change HP on one and wait up to 15 seconds: the other should update by itself.
  5. Count down together and tap Race test on both devices at the same moment. Afterwards both should show the same HP and history.
  6. Turn on airplane mode on one device, make a few changes, turn it off, and watch the changes save.
  7. On device B, tap Add test character in the Dan folder. On device A, tap Scan table and try to open it.
  8. Tap Copy report on each device and paste both reports into the chat.
  9. Round 2: tap Revoke access, switch to All of Drive, tap Clear log and counts, sign in, and repeat steps 3 to 8 with the same folder.
